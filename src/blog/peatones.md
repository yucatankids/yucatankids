>Peatones is an annual event celebrating architecture in Merida.  Developed and produced by Workshop Arquitectos ([www.workshop.com.mx](http://www.workshop.com.mx/)) and Vagantes (@vagantes.studio), the event showcases the best of houses in Merida and includes an after event at numerous best bars in Merida.  Peatones is Yucatan Kids AC largest sponsor.

>Thank you for your great efforts, Peatones Fest and to all the guests who will participate and support Yucatan Kids! Have a great time and share your pics with us.
